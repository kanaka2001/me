## 👥 Who is AWS Student Builder Group – DBIT?

**AWS Student Builder Group (SBG) – DBIT** is a student-driven cloud community at Don Bosco Institute of Technology, running in collaboration with the Department of AI & ML.

| What We Do | |
|---|---|
| 🛠️ **Workshops & Events** | Hands-on technical workshops and cloud lab sessions |
| 🔗 **Learning Resources** | Connecting students with AWS learning paths and career tools |
| 🤝 **Peer Learning** | Collaboration, community building, and knowledge sharing |
| 🚀 **Career Readiness** | Helping students build real skills for internships and placements |
