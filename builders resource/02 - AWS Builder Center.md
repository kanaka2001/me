## 🌐 What is AWS Builder Center?

AWS Builder Center is the official online home for the AWS community — where you learn, build, connect, and grow.

| Section | What You Get |
|---|---|
| 📚 **Learn** | Structured learning paths, training courses, and tutorials |
| 🔧 **Build** | Hands-on labs and project guides |
| 🌐 **Community** | Connect with builders globally, publish articles, join discussions |
| 🧰 **Toolbox** | SDKs, developer tools, and resources |

## 📋 Quick Steps: Create Your AWS Builder ID

Follow these steps after opening the sign-up link:

1️⃣ Click the link, click **Sign In**.

2️⃣ Enter your email, full name, and verify the **6-digit code** sent to your inbox.

3️⃣ Create a password and choose a professional, public **alias (username)**.

4️⃣ Select your **student status**.

5️⃣ Find your alias: Click your **profile icon** in the top right ➡️ **My Profile**.
